DropCount is a faithful recreation of the classic Drop7 puzzle game for Android, built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.<br>
<br>
<b>Core Gameplay</b><br>
<ul>
<li>7×7 Grid: Classic Drop7 gameplay with numbered and solid discs</li>
<li>Matches: Destroy discs by creating lengths of discs matching the disc numbers</li>
<li>Chain Reactions: Create cascading combos for higher scores</li>
<li>Dynamic Difficulty: New rows appear from the bottom based on game mode</li>
</ul>
<br>
<b>Game Modes</b><br>
<br>
1. Normal Mode<br>
<ul>
<li>New row every 30 drops, then 29, 28, 27... (decreasing)</li>
</ul>
<br>
2. Challenge Mode<br>
<ul>
<li>Configurable difficulty presets: Easy, Medium, Hard, Extreme</li>
<li>Fixed drop rate for fixed challenge</li>
</ul>
<br>
3. Sequence Mode<br>
<ul>
<li>Same rules as Normal mode</li>
<li>Deterministic disc generation for puzzle-solving</li>
<li>Predictable sequences for strategic planning</li>
</ul>
<br>
<b>Features</b><br>
<ul>
<li>High Score Tracking: Per-mode high score persistence</li>
<li>Beautiful UI: Modern Material 3 design with gradient backgrounds</li>
<li>Animations: Smooth disc drops and break effects</li>
<li>Pause/Resume: Pause the game anytime</li>
</ul>
<br>
<b>How to Play</b><br>
1. Tap a column to drop the current disc<br>
2. Match numbers: A disc breaks when contiguous discs in its row OR column equal its number<br>
3. Chain reactions: Breaking discs can trigger more breaks for bonus points<br>
4. Solid discs: Appear solid gray, crack once after first adjacent break, reveal a number after second adjacent break<br>
5. Survive: Avoid filling the past the top row<br>
<br>
DropCount is completely free and open source, with no ads or in-app purchases.
