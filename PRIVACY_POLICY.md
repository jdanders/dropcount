# Privacy Policy for DropCount

**DropCount** is an open-source, community-driven project. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

## 1. No Data Collection
DropCount is designed to be fully functional without collecting any personal data.
- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other identifying information.
- **No Tracking**: We do not use any analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising IDs.
- **No Cloud Sync**: Game data (high scores, statistics) is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to any server.

## 2. Information Handled Locally
- **Game Progress**: Your scores, statistics, and settings are stored locally on your device using Android's shared preferences.
- **Permissions**: This app requires no special permissions (e.g., location, camera, contacts) to operate.

## 3. Third Parties
DropCount does not share any information with third parties. It does not contain any third-party SDKs or libraries that collect data.

## 4. Open Source
The source code of DropCount is transparent and available for public audit at [github.com/jdanders/dropcount](https://github.com/jdanders/dropcount).

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the maintainers via the GitHub repository.
